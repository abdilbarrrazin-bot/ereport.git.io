about us

ereport is a modern digital platform designed to bridge communication between users and management. We believe that an efficient, well-maintained, and safe environment starts with an open and accessible line of communication. Through this platform, anyone can easily submit issue reports, facility damages, or maintenance needs in real-time. Every ticket submitted is tracked transparently—from initial submission to resolution—ensuring every issue gets handled efficiently.

User Guide & Help Center

Learn how to easily submit facility and issue reports in just a few steps.

1
Submit Your Report

Click on "Report an Issue" from the navigation bar. Fill in the title, precise location, category, and a clear description of the problem so our team can address it right away.

2
Helpful Tips

Always provide specific location details (e.g., Building B, 2nd Floor). Before creating a new report, feel free to check the View All Reports page to avoid submitting duplicate entries.
